# Plane Radar — Quick Start

Welcome! Your radar shows real aircraft flying near you, live. Setup takes
about two minutes and needs nothing but your home WiFi password.

## 1. Plug it in

Connect the USB-C cable to any USB power source (phone charger, laptop,
power bank). The screen lights up on its own.

## 2. Connect it to your WiFi

On first power-up the radar creates its own temporary WiFi network:

1. On your phone, open WiFi settings and join **PlaneRadar-Setup**.
2. A setup page opens automatically (if not, browse to **192.168.4.1**).
3. Pick your home WiFi network and enter its password.
4. Set your location — allow the location prompt and it fills itself in,
   or type coordinates. This is the centre of your radar.
5. Choose your preferred units and what info each plane's label shows.
6. Save. The radar restarts, joins your WiFi, and starts finding planes.

## 3. Using the radar

- **Rings** show distance (the range is written at the top-right).
- **Tap a plane** for its details and photo.
  - Tap the **photo** to see it bigger.
  - Tap the **QR code** to open the flight on your phone (FlightRadar24).
- **Press and hold a plane** to follow it — you'll see its trail.
- **Small dots at the rim** are planes just beyond the edge of your range.
- The **BOOT button** on the side cycles the radar range.

## 4. Settings (the green cog, bottom-right)

Sound alerts and volume, brightness, plane filters (e.g. hide airliners and
only see the interesting stuff), range, units, tag info, and more. Everything
takes effect immediately.

## 5. Updates

The radar checks for updates once a day and asks on-screen before
installing. You can also check manually: **Settings → System → Check for
update**. Updates are free and automatic — no computer needed, ever.

## Troubleshooting

- **Moved house / new WiFi?** Settings → System → Reset (clears WiFi and
  location, then walks you through setup again). Or hold the BOOT button
  for 3 seconds.
- **Device webpage:** browse to **http://plane-radar.local** from any device
  on the same WiFi for status, stats, and a live screenshot.
- **No planes showing?** Check the WiFi indicator; rural areas may genuinely
  have quiet skies — try a bigger range (BOOT button).
- **Start completely fresh:** Settings → System → Factory reset erases
  everything and returns the radar to out-of-the-box state.

Happy spotting! ✈️
